Floating Aquaculture & Plastic Solutions

Complete Floating Aquaculture Solutions

Planning, pontoon supply, cage systems, walkways, support structures and on-site installation.

Overview

Green Mart Fish & Agri provides practical floating infrastructure solutions for aquaculture projects. We work with customers to develop floating systems according to the water body, intended application, operating requirements and project scale.

Our solutions can combine modular HDPE floating pontoons, fish cage structures, access walkways, working platforms and floating support structures into one integrated system.

What We Provide

  • Site requirement assessment
  • Layout and configuration planning
  • HDPE pontoon supply
  • Cage and net-support infrastructure
  • Floating walkways and work platforms
  • Floating shelters/support structures
  • Accessories and connection components
  • On-site assembly support
  • Installation and commissioning assistance
  • Post-installation operational support

Applications

Reservoir aquaculture, lake-based fish farming, cage culture projects, private aquaculture farms and other suitable inland-water projects.

Green Mart Support

We combine aquaculture operating experience with the supply and setup of floating infrastructure. This helps us approach a project from the practical requirements of people who will actually operate the system — not simply as a plastic-product supply transaction.
