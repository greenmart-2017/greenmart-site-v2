From Components to an Operational Floating System

Layout, assembly, installation, checking and operational handover.

Overview

Green Mart can support customers beyond component supply by assisting with the practical setup of floating aquaculture infrastructure.

How It Works

Site Survey & PlanningCustom design based on your location and requirements
Supply & LogisticsHDPE pontoons and accessories organized for the approved layout
InstallationOn-site installation support by the project team
Testing & CommissioningChecks on connections, access, cage/net arrangements and supporting components
Handover & SupportGuidance and after-sales assistance after operational handover

During planning, the project requirements should be established first — including intended use, dimensions, water conditions, access requirements, cage arrangement and required support infrastructure.

Components are then organized according to the approved configuration. Modular sections can be assembled and connected before or during deployment depending on site conditions.

After installation, relevant connections, access sections, cage/net arrangements and supporting components should be checked before operational handover.

Technical / Project Considerations

For complex anchoring, structural, electrical or other specialist work, the final design and installation should be carried out or verified by appropriately qualified personnel.
