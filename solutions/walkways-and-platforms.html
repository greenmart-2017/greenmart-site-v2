Safer and More Organized Access on Water

Modular floating walkways and platforms connecting cages, shelters and working areas.

Overview

Access is a major operational requirement in floating aquaculture. Green Mart provides modular floating walkway and platform configurations that can connect cage units, shelters and working areas.

They can provide designated routes for routine activities such as feeding, cage inspection, net maintenance, sampling and general farm operations.

What We Provide

Configurations can include:

  • Straight access walkways
  • Cross/T-junction layouts
  • Perimeter walkways
  • Cage-to-cage connections
  • Working platforms
  • Equipment-access areas
  • Shelter-access platforms
  • Custom layouts

Where appropriate, systems can incorporate textured walking surfaces, handrails, ropes, edge protection or other safety features according to the project design.

Technical / Project Considerations

Design considerations include required width, expected operating load, water conditions, connection method, anchoring, worker movement, equipment movement, emergency access and future expansion.
