Floating Cage Systems for Modern Aquaculture

Configurable floating cage infrastructure for organized and scalable cage-based aquaculture.

Overview

Green Mart provides floating infrastructure for cage-based fish farming, integrating buoyancy modules, cage perimeter structures, net-support arrangements and operational access into a practical farming system.

A cage configuration should be selected according to the site rather than using one standard design everywhere. Factors such as water depth, water-level variation, wind and wave exposure, stocking plan, operational access, anchoring conditions and maintenance requirements should be considered during planning.

A Typical System May Include

  • Floating perimeter / pontoon structure
  • Fish containment net
  • Net-support and fastening arrangement
  • Access walkway
  • Connection hardware
  • Mooring / anchoring arrangement
  • Service platform
  • Optional floating shelter or feed-storage structure
⚠️ Stocking density, fish yield and cage capacity are not promised universally — these depend on species, cage dimensions, water quality, management and regulatory/site conditions.

Ideal For

  • Fresh water fish farming
  • Brackish water fish farming
  • Reservoirs, lakes & coastal areas
  • Government & private projects
  • Scalable from small to large farms
