Floating Aquaculture Solutions

Modular HDPE Floating Pontoon Systems

Modular floating units for aquaculture cages, walkways, platforms and other suitable floating structures.

Overview

Green Mart supplies modular HDPE floating pontoons for building configurable floating structures. Individual pontoon modules can be connected to create walkways, platforms and supporting layouts according to project requirements.

The modular approach makes it possible to expand, reconfigure or replace individual sections without necessarily rebuilding the complete floating structure.

Applications

Aquaculture cage systems, floating walkways, working platforms, access paths, floating shelters, maintenance platforms and other custom floating arrangements.

Key Characteristics

  • Modular configuration
  • Buoyant floating construction
  • Textured walking surface where applicable
  • Configurable dimensions
  • Replaceable individual modules
  • Suitable for repeated outdoor/water exposure, subject to product specification
  • Compatible with connecting accessories and anchoring arrangements
⚠️ Exact load capacity, UV life, material grade, wall thickness and service-life figures are not published here until confirmed against the manufacturer's technical datasheet — these vary by pontoon model.
