Our story

A family business, built on trust

Green Mart is a family-run business from Nagpur, Maharashtra — working in agriculture, fisheries and goods since 2017. What started as a local venture has grown into a dedicated aquaculture operation raising Pangasius (Basa) in our own farm.

We believe good food business is simple: honest weight, fresh stock, fair rates, and supply you can rely on. That's why we've formally applied for ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and HACCP certification — already received a positive initial response from an accredited certifying body — so every buyer, from a home kitchen to a hotel chain, will soon have full third-party assurance.

Now we're taking the next step — bringing locally processed, blast-frozen Basa fillet to Nagpur's stores and homes, so you never have to settle for months-old imported stock again.

Good to know

Common questions

What is the minimum order?
For businesses, bulk starts around 20–50 kg (negotiable). Home customers can order retail fillet packs once launched — enquire on WhatsApp for current minimums.
Which areas do you deliver to?
Nagpur city and surrounding region for fresh/live fish. Frozen fillet supply will expand wider as we grow — tell us your location on WhatsApp and we'll confirm.
How do payments work?
After we confirm your order and rate on WhatsApp, we send a secure Razorpay payment link — pay by UPI, card or net banking. Regular business buyers can discuss terms with us.
How do you maintain freshness?
Fresh fish moves iced the same day. Frozen fillet is blast-frozen fresh and held at −18°C with an unbroken cold chain till delivery — never thawed and refrozen.
How fast is delivery after ordering?
Fresh fish confirmed before 8 AM is usually delivered the same day within Nagpur; later orders go next morning. Frozen fillet delivery is scheduled with you on WhatsApp.
Do you ship frozen fillet outside Nagpur?
Yes for bulk orders — we use insulated boxes or reefer transport so the product stays at −18°C. Share your city on WhatsApp and we'll quote shipping. Retail-pack shipping to other cities is expanding gradually.
Is there a delivery charge?
Within Nagpur city, delivery is FREE on orders of 50 kg or more (₹80 flat below that). Outskirts and outstation have simple slab rates — use the delivery estimator above or confirm on WhatsApp.
Are you really certified?
We've formally applied for ISO 9001 (quality), ISO 14001 (environment) and HACCP (food safety) certification with an accredited body, and received a positive initial response. Certificates will be shared with business buyers as soon as they're issued.
Do you clean the fish, or is it sold whole?
Both — choose Whole Fish, Cleaned (scaled & gutted), or Curry Cut on most product pages. Just let us know your preference on WhatsApp when ordering.
How should I store the fish at home?
Fresh fish: refrigerate at 0–4°C and use within 1–2 days. Frozen fillet: keep at −18°C and do not refreeze once thawed — thaw only what you plan to cook.
Should I choose fresh or frozen?
Fresh is best if you're cooking within a day or two. Frozen fillet is ideal for stocking up — boneless, skinless, and locked at peak freshness via blast-freezing, so quality stays consistent for months.
